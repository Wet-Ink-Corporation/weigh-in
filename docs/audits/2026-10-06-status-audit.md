## Verdict

**Weigh-In is built and works end to end. v1 is now waiting mostly on Ryan's own time: a handful of hands-on checks and two weeks of real daily use that cannot finish before about 2026-10-19.**

The product described in the original design is built and working. Agents flag the calls they make, hooks capture those calls at no model cost, the /weigh-in sitting filters out what does not need you and puts the rest to you as short cards, and every answer is saved as a .kb decision record. The board page, originally planned for slice 2, also shipped. This repository has used Weigh-In on itself to record 14 of your decisions across 8 sittings, so the loop is real and not a demo. The code is in genuinely good shape: all 180 tests pass, there are zero lint findings, the core crate does no I/O as its charter says, and every build decision that affects the code was checked and is in place. v1 is not done yet because it was defined as proof that the product is useful in your daily work, and that proof has not been collected. The two weeks of real use (#23) only open on 2026-10-12. Three surface checks need you at a keyboard: Cowork session identity, the Cowork card, and Windows without Git Bash. You have not reviewed the eval labels. The newest install route, organisation sync, has not been confirmed to reach your machines. The status documents also lag the code: the roadmap still says 0.5.2 and contradicts itself. Few surprises remain on the build side. The real uncertainty is whether the two weeks of use show that people act on what Weigh-In surfaces, rather than dismissing it as noise. The realistic earliest v1 date is around 2026-10-19 to 10-21, and late October is more likely.

**Progress to v1: 63%.** Building is about 90% done and proof in real use about 36%; the headline is the plain average of the two. An equal 50/50 blend of two measures. (1) Planned build deliverables in v1-plan Phases 0 to 4: Phases 1 and 2 are done, Phase 0 is done except the rulesets, Phase 3's build tasks have shipped, and Phase 4 has done only its code review. I put this at about 90%. (2) The seven Phase 3 exit criteria in docs/v1-plan.md:311-324, scoring met as 1 and partial as 0.5: 1 met, 3 partial and 3 not met gives 2.5 of 7, about 36%. Blended: (90 + 36) / 2 ≈ 63%. Counting the evidence-based criteria alone, progress is about 36%. That is the fairer view of how much of v1's proof exists today. Ryan kept this figure on 2026-10-08 (wi-21f9d9).

**Remaining effort.** The build work left is small. The waiting is long. Agent build time to v1 is about 1.5 to 3 working days in total: about half a day to refresh the docs and decision index, half a day for the 1.0.0 release mechanics and v1-live record, and up to a day for the optional test, guard and hardening items. Your own hands-on time is about 2 to 4 hours, spread out: a Cowork check session (V1/V6), a Windows session without Git Bash, about 30 to 60 minutes reviewing labels, GitHub ruleset settings, confirming the org sync, and a few short decisions. The critical path is the two weeks of real use. Week 1 can be reported on or after 2026-10-12 and week 2 on or after 2026-10-19, later if 30 presented items take longer. The earliest realistic v1 date is therefore about 2026-10-20 to 10-22, and late October is more likely.
